    More Information

    🎯

    Objectives

    Study daily meals, breakfast habits, dietary diversity, packaged snacks, drinking water and food hygiene, then spread simple, affordable nutrition awareness.

    🧪

    Method

    Household questionnaire (45 sheets cross-checked), student interviews, visits to kirana stores and tiffin stalls, plus awareness sessions and leaflets.

    🏛️

    Local authorities

    Gram Panchayat, schools, village health workers and health departments can work together through regular programmes and follow-up surveys.

    📅

    Duration

    01 June 2026 to 26 July 2026, in Vidavalur Village, Vidavalur Mandal.
